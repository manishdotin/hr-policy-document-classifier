Employees may request annual leave through the employee portal. Requests should be submitted to the reporting manager for approval.

Business travel expenses must be submitted with itemized receipts within thirty days of returning from the trip.

Employees must record their start time, end time, and breaks using the attendance system each working day.

Eligible employees may work remotely on approved days after agreeing on availability and deliverables with their manager.

Open positions must have an approved requisition before a recruiter begins advertising or sourcing candidates.
